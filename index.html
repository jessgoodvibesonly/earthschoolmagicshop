A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Can I order yet?+

The Magic Shop is taking shape. You can explore the collection and save favourites now. Orders will open once product options, prices, and delivery details are confirmed. No payments are taken in this preview.

Do I need a game code to shop?+

No. The shop will be open to everyone. Player reward codes are planned as an extra way to discover eligible offers, with terms shown before purchase.

Can I redeem a real reward code now?+

Not yet. MAGIC-DEMO lets you try the experience, but does not create a discount or claim a reward. Live codes and their offer details will be available when the rewards programme opens.

Where are my favourites saved?+

Your wishlist is stored in this browser on this device. It does not create an account, reserve stock, or place an order. Clearing browser data removes it.
